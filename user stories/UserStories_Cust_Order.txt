Customer
1.As a news agent, I want to create a new customer because a new local customer wants me to deliver newspapers.
2.As a news agent, I want to update a customer's details so that any deliveries are delivered to them correctly.
3.As a news agent, I want to be able to see a customer's details to ensure if they are correct.
4.As a news agent, I want to delete a customer's account as they no longer wish to receive newspapers regularly.

Orders
1.As a news agent, I want to place an order for newspapers to be delivered as the customer has asked to receive newspapers regularly.
2.As a news agent, I want to be able to see what publications the customer is receiving because I want to remind myself of what publications they ordered.
3.As a news agent, I want to update an order because a customer has changed their mind on what publication to receive.
As a news agent, I want to delete an order as the customer accidentally ordered a publication.

AC:
Cust:
1.User enters all required details and create, then the customer will be added to the system.
  The customer will have a unique CustID.
  Required fields such as name, address and contact details must not be empty.
  The system should display a confirmation alert when the customer is successfully created.
  Invalid info should display an error alert.
2.User selects the customer, edits their details, then the updated info is saved.
  The system has to allow changes to details.
  The updated details should be displayed when selected again.
  The system should display a confirmation alert to confirm that the update was successful.
3.If customer exists in the system, the user selects a customer, their details are displayed.
  The displayed info should be; CustID, name, address and contact details.
  The details displayed must match the information stored.
  If the customer does not exist, the system should display an alert.
4.User selects a customer's account to delete . 
  A confirmation message should display to confirm the deletion.
  The deleted customer should no longer appear in the system.
  The system should display confirmation that the customer was successfully deleted.
  If the user tries to delete a customer who does not exist, an error message should display.
