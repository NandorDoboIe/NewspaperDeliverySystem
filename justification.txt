Incremental Model Justification

We are choosing an incremental development model for our Newspaper Delivery system. We are a team of 4 students working together for the first time. 
An incremental approach allows us to build in smaller stages so we can review and improve as we code.

We believe we will encounter additional requirements or attributes that the finished product may need.
There may be alternative ways to implement the requirements.

Every increment we make to the system will allow us to review the features, find ways we can improve on them and improve them if we can. (Successfully)
Iterative model is also appropriate for our team as we develop an understanding of our strengths and weaknesses as well as our communication styles.
